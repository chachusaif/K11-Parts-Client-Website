    Why buy it from me

    Buying K11 parts out of Japan is mostly about knowing which car a thing came off and whether it will actually bolt to yours.

    I know what's JDM-only

    The Japanese March got trim, clusters and Autech variants that never reached the UK. Knowing what exists is half the job — a lot of owners don't realise the part they want was fitted as standard on the other side of the world.

    Fitment gets checked first

    Early K11 and facelift K11C parts aren't always interchangeable, and neither are Micra and March equivalents. I check what your car is before anything gets ordered, so you're not left with a panel that doesn't line up.

    Honest condition, in writing

    Used Japanese parts are used parts. You get photos of the actual item, the marks and the wear included, and a straight answer on whether it needs work before it goes on the car.
